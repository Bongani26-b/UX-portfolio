Treppers

Treppers is a mobile-first community platform that connects entrepreneurs with products, services, and each other in one place.

Role

Lead UI/UX Designer (Solo)

Client

Internal project

Status

Concept / Not Live

Tools

Figma

Platform

Mobile

01 — Project Overview

Treppers is a community-based platform designed to connect entrepreneurs, service providers, and product seekers in one ecosystem. The goal was to create a space where users could easily discover, offer, and request services or products while building meaningful professional connections. This project was an internal initiative, and I was responsible for the entire UI/UX process — from early research and ideation to final high-fidelity designs. Although the product is not live, it was designed with real-world scalability and usability in mind.

02 — The Problem

Entrepreneurs often struggle with:

  • Finding trusted service providers and relevant products
  • Navigating multiple platforms for networking, hiring, and selling
  • Lack of community-driven discovery and recommendations

Existing platforms tend to focus on either marketplaces or communities rarely both resulting in fragmented experiences.

03 — The Goal

Design a platform that:

01

Combines community + marketplace into a single experience

02

Makes it easy to discover services and products

03

Encourages collaboration and trust among entrepreneurs

04

Feels simple, intuitive, and welcoming for first-time users

04 — Research Methods

Since this was an internal project, I conducted light but focused research, including:

  • Informal interviews with entrepreneurs and freelancers
  • Online research on similar platforms (e.g., community marketplaces)
  • Competitive analysis to identify gaps and opportunities


Key Insights

  • Users want speed — they don’t want to browse endlessly
  • Trust is critical when choosing services or products
  • Clear categorization and filtering are essential
  • Users value community validation (profiles, activity, engagement)

06 — Personas


Primary Persona: The Entrepreneur

  • Owns or is building a business
  • Needs services (design, marketing, development, etc.)
  • Wants reliable and vetted providers
  • Values efficiency and clarity

Secondary Persona: The Service Provider

  • Offers services or products
  • Wants visibility and credibility
  • Needs an easy way to showcase offerings

07 — Information Architecture

I focused on keeping navigation simple and predictable:


    Core Sections:

  • Sign in / Register
  • Profile creation
  • Home / Feed
  • Marketplace (Products & Services)

The structure was designed to minimize cognitive load and help users reach their goal in as few steps as possible.
